Privacy Policy

Connie's Skin · Effective date: May 8, 2026

Connie's Skin ("the app") is a skincare routine companion operated by Charles Gao ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, password (stored as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and an optional display name.
  • Skin profile and quiz answers: the skin type, concerns, sensitivity, budget, and routine-size preferences you choose in the product quiz, and the sensitivity profile you pick during onboarding.
  • Routine data: the products, routines, weekly schedule, and completion history you create in the app.
  • Photos: product photos you choose to attach from your photo library. The app only accesses photos you explicitly select.
  • Purchase information: if you buy a routine kit, our payment processor (Stripe) handles your payment details. We never see or store your full card number. We keep a record of what you ordered and its status so we can fulfill it.
  • Notifications: your reminder time preferences, stored so the app can schedule local reminders on your device.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use your information

  • To sync your routines, products, and progress across your devices.
  • To generate routine suggestions and ingredient-conflict warnings from the data you enter.
  • To process and fulfill routine kit orders you place.
  • To send the daily routine reminders you configure (scheduled locally on your device).
  • To respond when you contact us for support.

Who processes your data

Your data is stored and processed by two service providers acting on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage for your account and routine data.
  • Stripe — payment processing for kit purchases (Stripe's privacy policy).

We do not sell your personal information or share it with anyone else, except if required by law.

A note on skincare data

Your skin profile and routine history describe cosmetic preferences, not medical conditions. The app's warnings are conservative skincare guidance, not medical diagnosis or treatment. We treat this data with the same care as everything else and never use it for advertising or profiling.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, routines, products, photos, quiz answers, and order history are removed from our systems. Payment records held by Stripe are retained as required by financial regulations.

You can also export your data anytime from Settings as a JSON file.

Children

Connie's Skin is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. The in-app export and account deletion cover most of these directly; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and note it in the app. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version.
