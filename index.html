Inside the app

For the routine you actually use.

Today & Plan

Know what comes next.

Open Today to see your morning or evening routine. Check off each product as you go, then use Plan to move things around for the week.

Connie also checks a few routine patterns, including retinoid and acid combinations and mornings without an SPF step.

Your products

Keep your products together.

Keep your products in My Shelf, save others for later, and compare two side by side.

You can check ingredient lists and follow links to their sources. When a brand makes a product claim, Connie shows you who said it.

Your week

Give each day a plan.

Use the same routine every day, or choose different ones through the week. Your morning and evening schedules are separate, so changing one leaves the other in place.

Over in Progress, you can see completed routines on a calendar and add diary notes.

iPhone app screens. Routine and product examples are sample data.

Why it’s free

You don’t need another subscription.

Connie’s Skin is free to use. Our business model is selling optional skincare products. You can use what you already own or buy elsewhere; a purchase doesn’t unlock extra app features.

Shopping availability varies by app version. The optional skin scan is available once every seven days.

About the optional scan

You can skip the selfie.

Your answers are enough to get started. If you choose to scan, the app explains where your photo goes and asks for your consent each time.

Results stay with your accountA prepared photo can stay in the app’s cache on your device for comparisons. It doesn’t sync to your other devices.

A few questions

Before you start.

Connie’s Skin is for adults aged 18 and over.

Do I have to buy new products?+

No. Add products you already own and build your routine around them.

Do I need to take a selfie?+

No. You can continue without a scan and use your answers to set up a routine.

Is this medical advice?+

No. Connie’s Skin provides cosmetic information and routine tools. Scan results are estimates, not a diagnosis. For medical concerns, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

Can I delete my account?+

Yes. Open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. Contact support if you can’t access the app.
