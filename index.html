Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, including routines, photos, and quiz answers.

How do I reset my password?
On the login screen, enter your email and tap "Forgot password?" — a reset link will be sent to your inbox.

Where's my kit order?
Email us with the address you used at checkout and we'll send your order status.

Is the ingredient guidance medical advice?
No — warnings are conservative skincare guidance, not diagnosis or treatment. For skin conditions, please see a dermatologist.
