Inside the app

A little more confidence in your skincare.

Today & Plan

Make the daily part easier.

Some mornings, you just want to know which bottle comes next. Your routine is ready in Today, with each step there to check off.

If you put a retinoid and an acid in the same routine, or leave SPF out of a morning routine, Connie flags it so you can take another look.

Your products

Look past the front of the bottle.

Before adding something new, see what you already have. Compare two products side by side and read their ingredient lists in one place.

When a brand says a product does something, you can read the claim and follow the source.

Your week

Keep going after a missed evening.

A late night doesn’t have to mean starting over. Keep tomorrow’s routine in view and adjust the week when your plans change.

Your calendar and diary give you a record of what you did and how your skin felt. You can look back whenever you’re wondering what changed.

Why it’s free

You don’t need another subscription.

We make money from optional skincare purchases. The app is free, whether you shop with us, use what you own, or buy elsewhere. Buying something doesn’t unlock extra features.

Shopping availability varies by app version. The optional skin scan is available once every seven days.

About the optional scan

You can skip the selfie.

You can get started just by answering a few questions. If you want to try a scan, you’ll see where your photo goes before you share it. The app asks for your consent every time.

Photo comparisons stay on this deviceThe app may keep a prepared copy of your photo in its cache for comparisons. That copy doesn’t sync to other devices. Scan results sync with your account.
Google Gemini processes the imageGoogle’s abuse-monitoring policy includes 55 days of retention for scan data. Read the details before sharing a photo.

A few questions

Before you start.

Connie’s Skin is for adults aged 18 and over.

Do I have to buy new products?+

No. Add products you already own and build your routine around them.

Do I need to take a selfie?+

No. You can continue without a scan and use your answers to set up a routine.

Is this medical advice?+

No. Connie’s Skin provides cosmetic information and routine tools. Scan results are estimates, not a diagnosis. For medical concerns, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

Can I delete my account?+

Yes. Open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. Contact support if you can’t access the app.
