How to organize the skincare products you already own

If your shelf is crowded but your skincare still feels uncertain, take stock before adding another bottle.

By Connie’s Skin · Published October 9, 2026

Open the bathroom drawer. Check the travel bag. There's probably something you haven't thought about in a while.

Put the products you own on one list so you can see what's in use and what's waiting in a cupboard. Paper is fine. Here's a sample entry with a made-up product name:

Example Brand Daily Cleanser, 150 ml
In use · bathroom shelf
Opened October 9
One unopened spare in the drawer

Four lines, or four spreadsheet columns. You can add detail later if you keep looking for something the list doesn't tell you.

  1. Get the names right

    Keep the bottles and packaging beside you while you write. Include spares and samples. Record the brand and full product name; add the size if you have more than one version.

    “Cleanser, 30 ml, travel bag” will still make sense in a month. “The little blue one” might not.

  2. Separate what you use from what you might buy

    Give products in use and unopened spares their own headings. Keep a wishlist elsewhere. A saved product photo can look a lot like something you've already bought when you're scrolling quickly.

    If your products live in more than one place, note where. You can leave the gym-bag cleanser in the gym bag.

  3. Record the details you tend to forget

    An opening date can be useful, as can “spare bottle in top drawer.” If you don't remember a date, write “unknown.”

    Keep the product label for its storage and use instructions. Copying an opening date into a list doesn't tell you whether a product is still suitable to use.

  4. Change the list when you change the shelf

    Finished a bottle? Remove it. Opened the spare? Move it into the in-use group. Before buying a replacement, check the list and the cupboard, especially if it's been a while since you updated either.

Keeping your shelf in Connie's Skin

Go to Library → My Shelf and tap Add to record a product you own. The shelf also shows where it appears in your routines, so you can refer to it when you plan your week.

Use Saved for products you've bookmarked in Discover and want to look at again. You'll need to sign in to use Saved. Those bookmarks stay separate from My Shelf.
