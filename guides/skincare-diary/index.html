How to keep a skincare diary

Some days your skin feels better than others. A few dated notes give you something to look back at, beyond how things seem today.

By Connie’s Skin · Published October 9, 2026

You might remember buying a new moisturizer. Remembering whether that was last Tuesday or three weeks ago is harder. A skincare diary gives those details a date.

Use somewhere you already look: a notebook by the bed, a note on your phone, or an app. A few lines are enough.

Copy this into your first entry

Date:
What I used:
How my skin felt:
Anything different today:
Something to remember:

Skip any line you don't need. “What I used” can be the name of your usual routine; write out the product name when you change something or need to tell two similar bottles apart.

A made-up entry might look like this:

October 9. Usual morning and evening routines. Cheeks felt tight after washing tonight. Opened a new bottle of my usual moisturizer. Late night yesterday.

Leave room for ordinary days

Most entries won't have much to say. “Same routine, skin felt comfortable” is a useful note. So is “Forgot to write this down yesterday.”

Try writing after your evening routine, while you still remember the day. If you miss a week, carry on with today's date. You can leave a gap. If you do add an older entry, say what you remember and leave the rest blank.

Look back for one thing at a time

Start with a question your notes can answer: “When did I open this?” or “What was I using last week?” Look at the dates and the words you actually wrote.

A diary can't tell you why your skin changed. A product change and a good skin day might fall close together without one causing the other. Keep those observations as observations.

You might also notice that the evening routine you planned rarely fits your evenings. That gives you something concrete to consider when you look at your weekly plan.

Where to write it in Connie's Skin

Open Progress, choose Routine, and tap a date on the calendar. Write your note, then tap Save note. You can add tags such as New product, Travel, or Late night.

Tap the date again whenever you want to read or edit the entry. There's room for 1,000 characters per note.
