About these guides

Connie’s Skin makes tools for planning routines and keeping track of products. These guides cover those everyday tasks. They don’t prescribe treatments or tell you which products will suit your skin.

We use AI to help draft and edit, and check app instructions against the current features. Examples are made up; app screens show sample data. If a step looks different in your version, contact support.
