About these guides

These guides are for the everyday work of looking after your skin: remembering what you used, making time for your routine, and keeping track when something changes. Use them with a notebook, a spreadsheet, or Connie’s Skin. They cover planning and recordkeeping, not treatment advice.

We use AI to help draft and edit. We check app instructions against the current features; the examples and app screens use sample data. If a step looks different in your version, contact support.
