How to plan your morning and evening skincare

You want better skin. You also need to get out the door in the morning. Put your routine somewhere you can follow it without working it out again each day.

By Connie’s Skin · Published October 9, 2026

Start with a morning and evening you know well. Write down the products you already use, in the order you use them. A sheet of paper with AM on one side and PM on the other is enough.

Use the names on the bottles. “Moisturizer” works if you own one; if you own three, be specific. Check your list against the shelf, or make a quick product inventory first.

For order, frequency, and whether a product suits you, follow its instructions and any advice from your clinician. This is a way to record that routine.

Give it a place in the week

If your mornings are the same, give that routine a name and reuse it. If your evening routine changes across the week, give each version a name you'll recognize. “Monday evening” is fine if that's what makes sense to you.

Add the other days you want to plan. Match the schedule to the product instructions you're following, including how often to use each one.

Put it into Connie's Skin

Open Plan and select a day. Find your routine under AM routines or PM routines, then tap Assign beside it.

Tap a routine to see its steps or edit it from its detail screen. When the day comes, open Today. The AM and PM tabs show what's assigned, ready to check off as you finish.

When the week doesn't go to plan

Some evenings you'll get home late. Sometimes the travel bag will still be in the other suitcase. Mark what you actually did, and leave the rest unchecked.

If the same problem keeps coming up, add a short diary note. A note like “Left this bottle at home again” can remind you to pack it next time.
