Ingredient reference

Ethylhexylglycerin

Listed in 37 products in our library. Read the register’s reported functions and follow the source.

What does the CosIng register report?

The European Commission’s CosIng register lists the following functions for ETHYLHEXYLGLYCERIN. The function names and definitions below reproduce the register’s wording.

Reported function: DEODORANT

Reducing and/or eluminating unpleasant odour. Contributing against the formation of malodour on body surfaces.

Source: European Commission, CosIng

Reported function: SKIN CONDITIONING

Maintaining the skin in good condition.

Source: European Commission, CosIng

What does that mean for a product?

A reported ingredient function is not a finding that a finished product delivers that result. This reference does not establish safety, efficacy or whether a product suits your skin.
