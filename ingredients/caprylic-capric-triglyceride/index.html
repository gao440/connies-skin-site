Ingredient reference

Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride

Listed in 19 products in our library. Read the register’s reported functions and follow the source.

What does the CosIng register report?

The European Commission’s CosIng register lists the following functions for CAPRYLIC/CAPRIC TRIGLYCERIDE. The function names and definitions below reproduce the register’s wording.

Reported function: FRAGRANCE

Imparting an odour or taste. Creating a perceivable pleasant smell and/or masking a bad smell.

Source: European Commission, CosIng

Reported function: PERFUMING

Used for perfume and aromatic raw materials.

Source: European Commission, CosIng

Reported function: SKIN CONDITIONING - OCCLUSIVE

Retarding the evaporation of water from the skin surface. Occlusives are generally lipids which tend to remain on the skin surface and thus increase the water content of skin.

Source: European Commission, CosIng

Reported function: SOLVENT

Dissolving other components of cosmetics. Solvents are usually liquids (aqueous and nonaqueous).

Source: European Commission, CosIng

What does that mean for a product?

A reported ingredient function is not a finding that a finished product delivers that result. This reference does not establish safety, efficacy or whether a product suits your skin.
