Our sources

Know where the
information comes from.

The library brings ingredient lists and reference sources into one place, with the limits of those records kept in view.

What is in the first edition?

This edition includes 53 products across 13 brands, selected from the public Connie’s Skin catalogue. Each has a recorded ingredient declaration and a source link. Inclusion isn’t an endorsement, a quality ranking, or confirmation that a product is available to buy.

Ingredient lists and product information

Product names, brands and ingredient declarations come from the sources linked on each product page. Some ingredient evidence comes from a separately identified retailer page. Product types use our existing catalogue categories. We preserve the recorded ingredient text and the order of its parsed names.

Check your packaging for the formula you have: names, regional versions and formulas can change. A linked ingredient reference identifies a recorded ingredient; it does not predict a product’s result or tell you an ingredient’s concentration.

Reported ingredient functions

Our ingredient pages attribute function names and definitions to the European Commission’s CosIng register. We reproduce that wording verbatim under CC BY 4.0, with links to the source. Only the layout is changed. A register entry is not approval of a finished product or evidence of its safety or efficacy.

Dates mean different things

  • Added to the website: when we published a page here.
  • Source record updated: the timestamp recorded for the upstream product record. It does not mean we rechecked its ingredient list.
  • Ingredient evidence retrieved: when a separately recorded ingredient source was captured, where that date exists.
  • Public catalogue availability checked: when we confirmed that the record was available through the app’s public catalogue.

The public catalogue snapshot used for this edition was checked October 30, 2026. That availability check is separate from our source audit. For each included product, we also compared the complete recorded ingredient declaration with its source, including the start and end of the list. The date of that comparison appears on the product page. Capitalization, spacing and punctuation may differ. This does not guarantee that every regional version or later bottle has the same formula.

Corrections and updates

If a name, ingredient list or link needs correcting, email support@connieskin.com with the product page and a source or packaging reference. We update records when we have new evidence; dates are not refreshed just to make a page look new.

Connie’s Skin provides cosmetic information and routine tools, not medical advice. Brand product names and any statements on linked brand pages belong to their respective brands.
